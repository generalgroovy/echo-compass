How it works

The same tone is panned between your left and right audio channels. The diagram shows stereo balance, not physical angles or distance. Listen again as often as you like; Stop cancels an unfinished listen.

Fine differences depend on headphones, hearing, volume and browser output. This is a playful attention exercise, not a hearing test or a calibrated spatial-audio model. It does not use a microphone.

Only settings save in this browser. Rounds and totals restart on reload. Audio always starts with Listen and stops when this tab hides.
